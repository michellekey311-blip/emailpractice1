Step 2: Prompt and sentence starters

You received an email from your friend, Jessie.

Hi,

The school holidays are coming soon! My family and I are planning to go on a holiday.

Should we go to the beach or the mountains? Why would you recommend that place? What activities can we do there? What should we bring for the trip?

I look forward to your reply.

Jessie

Write an email to Jessie in about 80 words. Answer all the questions in her email.
